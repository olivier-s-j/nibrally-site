Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to info@nibrally.com. A screenshot helps.

Report a problem from the game

Open Profile, then Settings, and tap Report a problem. Or tap Something wrong? under any match result. Your email app opens with a short message to us and a few details about the game: its version, your device, the language and where you are on the ladder. You can read and change everything before you send it. Tick the box to add your save code if you want us to see your exact game.

Switch between treble, bass or both

Open Profile, then Settings, and choose Clef. Each clef keeps its own ladder, so you can switch back any time. The notes you already know come along.

Skip ahead if you already read notes

Open Profile and tap Take the placement test. It can place you as high as Duke. Beat that class's boss to make the rank official.

Change the language or note names

Open Profile, then Settings. Choose your language from the list, and your note names: C D E, Do Re Mi with Si or Ti, German names with H, or the names used in your language.

Several players on one device

Open Profile, tap Players, then Add a player. Give them a name if you like and pick a colour for their Nib. Each player has their own progress. With two or more players the game asks Who's playing? when it starts, and Switch player on the main page changes player any time. Tap the pencil on a player to rename them or delete them; deleting a player deletes their progress on this device.

See practice time and send a report to the teacher

Open Profile and tap Practice history. It shows the last 7 or 30 days: minutes of practice per day, how many notes are known, how often notes were right the first time, and which notes need work. After a lesson, tap Mark today as lesson day, and next time Since the lesson shows everything since then. Share report for teacher makes a one-page image you can send by message or email, or print. Practice time is an estimate: it counts while a note is waiting for an answer and stops 30 seconds after the last answer.

Move your progress to another device

On the old device, open Profile, then Backup, and tap Copy save code. On the new device, tap Restore in the same place and paste the code. A code holds the progress of whoever is playing, with their name and Nib colour. With several players, do this once for each player, while that player is playing.

Turn off vibration or sound

Open Profile, then Settings. Switch Vibration off, or slide Sound down.

Start over

Open Profile and tap Reset progress. This clears the progress of whoever is playing; other players keep theirs, and your settings stay.
