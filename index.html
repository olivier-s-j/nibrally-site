Read music by playing

A note glides across the staff towards Nib. Name it on the piano before your rival does, and Nib eats it. One new note at a time, from your very first note to every line and space.

How a match works

  1. A note sets off

    It glides along the staff towards Nib, while your rival gets ready to read it too.

  2. You name it

    Tap its name on the piano before your rival's timer runs out. A quick answer scores double.

  3. Nib eats it, or your rival grabs it

    First to 12 points wins the round, two rounds win the match. Miss a note and its name is shown, so every note teaches you something.

Treble, bass or both

The game asks what you play and gives you your own ladder. Switch any time in Settings; the notes you know come along.

Climb seven classes

Every rival brings exactly one new note. Every class ends with a boss who changes one rule. Beat the Dragon to become a Legend.

  1. PagePage Patient. Slow, but always right.
  2. SquireSquire Steady. Every answer is worth one.
  3. KnightKnight Mirror. Same distance, different clefs.
  4. BaronBaron Hasty. Fast feet, plenty of mistakes.
  5. DukeDuke Switch. Check the clef each time.
  6. KingKing Ledger. Read beyond the staff.
  7. DragonDragon Rising. A little faster each round.
  8. LegendEvery note on your staff, read like a pro.

Already read some notes? A short placement test starts you higher, up to Duke.

Notes that stay

Inside the game

For parents and teachers

Who it is for
Children from about 8, and adult beginners. A good companion to piano lessons, music school or choir.
Private by design
No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Progress, names and practice history stay on the device and the game never goes online.
Note names your way
Letters (C D E), Do Re Mi with Si or Ti, German names with H, or the names used in your language.
At your own pace
Practice without a clock, a free hint whenever you need one, and no penalty for losing a match.
A report for the teacher
Minutes of practice per day, the notes they know and the ones to work on, sent as a one-page image whenever you like.
The whole family
Up to four players on one device, each with their own progress and their own colour of Nib.
