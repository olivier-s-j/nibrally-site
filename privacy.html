Privacy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Nib Rally does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads. The app has no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The only code from others in it is the purchase code of Apple and Google, which handles the purchase of the full game with your store account.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Your ladder, the notes you learned, gold, settings and practice history (which days you practised, for how long, and how each match or practice went) are saved only on your device. Like any app's data, they can be part of your device's own backup to iCloud or Google, which only you can access. If you use "Copy save code", the code (one player's progress, name and Nib colour) is made on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.
  • Players and names. Up to four players can share one device, each with their own progress. A player's name is optional, is typed on the device and stays there. It is never sent to us.
  • Report for a teacher. "Share report for teacher" makes an image on your device with the player's name (if there is one), practice time and how well they read each note. Your device's share sheet sends it only where you choose, for example to a teacher. We never see it.
  • No internet needed. The game works offline and sends nothing to us. Only the purchase of the full game, and the check that it is yours, go through the App Store or Google Play.
  • The full game. One purchase in the App Store or on Google Play opens the full game. Apple or Google handle the purchase with your store account, and Solai BV receives no payment details. Before the store's purchase screen opens, a grown-up answers a short sum. The game remembers on your device that the full game is open, and a "Report a problem" email says so.
  • Email. If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we never share them. We keep them only as long as we need them to help you, and we delete them whenever you ask. "Report a problem" in the game only prepares an email in your own email app. It adds the app version, device type, language and ladder position, the last error messages the game noted on your device (technical text such as "SyntaxError at line 1582", nothing personal), a short log of the game's sound (when it started, stopped and came back as you switched apps, with the time of day), and your save code only if you tick the box. If the game ever cannot start, a "Something wrong?" screen offers the same kind of email. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • Children. Because nothing is collected, the app is suitable for children. The three things that can take a player's name or save out of the app ("Report a problem", "Copy save code" and "Share report for teacher") first ask a grown-up to answer a short sum.
