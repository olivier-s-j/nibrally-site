Privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Nib Rally does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Your ladder, the notes you learned, gold and settings are saved only on your device. Like any app's data, they can be part of your device's own backup to iCloud or Google, which only you can access. If you use "Copy save code", the code is made on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.
  • No internet needed. The game works offline. It does not send anything to us or anyone else.
  • Email. If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we never share them. "Report a problem" in the game only prepares an email in your own email app. It adds the app version, device type, language and ladder position, and your save code only if you tick the box. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • Children. Because nothing is collected, the app is suitable for children.
