Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Nib Rally: Workbook does not collect or share any personal data. What it keeps stays on your iPad.

  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no code from anyone else that collects data. It makes no connection to the internet at all.
  • Your progress stays on your iPad. The app keeps, on the iPad only: the learners' names, each learner's level and how their recent notes went, the tunes they found, a date and the notes of the closing melody for every page they finished, and your settings. Like any app's data, this can be part of your device's own backup to iCloud, which only you can reach. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
  • Names. A learner's name is optional, is typed on the iPad and stays there. It is never sent to us.
  • Your handwriting is not kept. Notes you place or write are checked on the iPad. Handwriting is read on the iPad and then discarded; the ink is not saved. Of a finished page, only the notes of its closing melody are kept.
  • A panel for our testers. The app contains tools for the people who test it, hidden unless someone opens them on purpose. While they are open, handwriting is kept on the iPad as samples, each with the iPad's model and iPadOS version, and the person holding the iPad can export them with the share sheet to wherever they choose. The samples stay on the iPad until the app is deleted. Nothing is sent to us by the app.
  • The help link. Help and contact in Settings opens this website in your browser. Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting service (GitHub Pages), which sees the address your device connects from.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and asks for no personal information.

Solai BV, Belgium, makes the app. Questions: info@nibrally.com. If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
