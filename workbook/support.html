Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to info@nibrally.com. A screenshot helps.

Place a note

One column of the staff is marked in blue. Touch it, slide up or down to the line or space you want, and lift. The next column turns blue. A finger and an Apple Pencil both work.

Change a note

Touch the notehead and drag it to another place, before or after Check. You can also tap a note's name above the staff to make its column blue again, or tap Undo to take back the last note.

Start higher if you already read notes

On the first launch, choose I can read notes already and pick a level. Later, open Settings (the gear) and change Level.

Stay at a level, or go back

When a page earns the next note, choose Add or Stay at this level. On the finish screen, the minus and plus beside the level take you to any easier level and back up to the highest one you reached.

Do Re Mi or C D E

Open Settings and choose under Note names.

Write by hand with Apple Pencil

Open Settings and choose Write with Apple Pencil under How to answer. You then draw each notehead and the app reads it. This needs an Apple Pencil; a finger does not write. The eraser, or a double-tap on the Pencil, removes ink.

A bigger or smaller staff

Open Settings and choose under Staff size. On a small iPad the staff is made to fit the screen.

Several learners on one iPad

Open Settings, type a name under Who is writing and tap Add. Tap a name to switch. Each learner has their own level, tunes and stamps.

The hidden tunes

The last exercise of a page is a short melody. Sometimes it is the opening of a well-known tune, and the next line follows on a later page. The finish screen shows how many you have found.

Start over

Add a new learner in Settings to begin again at the first note. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the iPad.
