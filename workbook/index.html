Nib Rally: Workbook

A music workbook for iPad that never runs out of pages. Each exercise names a few notes, you put them on the staff in the treble or bass clef, and every note is checked.

Get it on the App Store

Place the notes

Touch a column on the staff, slide to the line or space, and lift. A finger and an Apple Pencil both work, and a note in the wrong place can be picked up and moved. Have an Apple Pencil and want to really write? Choose handwriting and draw each notehead yourself.

Every note is checked

Each exercise asks for exact notes: the clef, the name and the octave. Check marks every note right or wrong, says what you placed and what it should be, and shows where it belongs.

One new note at a time

You start with middle C and one note on the lower staff. Pages you get right earn the next note, over both staves and their ledger lines: 28 levels and 29 notes. Add the new note or stay at your level for as long as you like, and go back to an easier one whenever you want.

Hidden tunes

Every page ends with a short melody to write. Now and then it turns out to be the opening of a tune you know. There are 21 to find.

Who it is for

For learners of any age who want to write music notes and not only read them. It goes well with piano lessons, music school, choir or a music theory class. The app speaks 44 languages and names the notes the way your language does: C D E, Do Re Mi, H for B, or in your own script.

Private and complete

One price, everything included. No ads, no accounts, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your iPad and the app works offline.

A separate app from Nib Rally

Nib Rally is the game that teaches reading notes. The Workbook is for writing them. Each works on its own.
